Doing uninterrupted sessions may make you feel like you are doing the best thing for your body.
But not giving yourself a break between workouts will mean that you will burn out, and do more harm
than good. You should be taking a day off from exercising every two to three days as a minimum

of time your muscles are tense for and increase the blood flow.
